Game-jam demo · Godot 4.5

MAG REVVIN

A compressed-air thruster car loose in an overgrown cyberpunk city. Boost, jump, fly on six jets, flip it, ride the walls, and outrun the beasts.

▶ Play in your browser Source on GitHub

Works on desktop and on phones (hold it sideways). About a 13 MB download the first time; after that your browser keeps it cached.

What's in the demo

The car is a real physics model, not a sprite on rails: tyres, suspension, four rear thrusters, six perimeter jets, suction fans and the tanks and battery that feed them.

Thrust and jets

Boost on the rear thrusters, tap to jump, tap again to double jump or dodge-flip, hold to fly. A 200 L and a 400 L tank of air, refilled by a compressor.

Style score

Flips, barrel rolls, corkscrews, big air, wall rides and drifts. Every landing is graded from PERFECT to ROUGH, and banked tricks pump air back into your tanks.

SKYWAY course

A stunt course with loops, hoops, a half-pipe and checkpoints. Set a lap time, then try to beat it.

A city gone wild

Ruined arcology towers, glowing tech-trees, deer that bolt and birds that scatter. Scenery you can smash, and bosses that hunt you down.

Controls

The layout follows Rocket League. On a phone, the controls appear on screen as soon as the game loads.

Left thumbFloating stick: steer. In the air it pitches the nose and yaws.
Drag the carGrab the car and it tips the way you drag: up for a front flip, down for a back flip, sideways for a barrel roll, diagonals mix. On the road, the drag jumps first.
HoldKeep your finger down to hold the car at that angle. Twist a second finger around the first to spin it flat.
FlickLet go mid-swipe and the car keeps spinning at the speed of your flick.
Right thumbGAS, BRAKE (reverse when stopped), JUMP (tap twice to double jump, hold to fly), BOOST, DRIFT.
Anywhere elseDrag to look around. Top left: pause, camera, recover, fullscreen.
W / SThrottle, brake and reverse. In the air: pitch.
A / DSteer. In the air: yaw.
Space / right mouseJump. Again in the air: double jump, or dodge-flip with a direction held. Hold to fly.
Left mouse / CtrlBoost.
ShiftPowerslide. In the air, hold it and steer to roll.
Q / EAir roll. On the road: side jets.
MouseLook around. B or middle mouse looks back.
V · R · EscCamera · recover upright · pause menu and settings.
F1Full key list in game.
RT / LTThrottle, brake and reverse.
Left stickSteer. In the air: pitch and yaw.
AJump, double jump, dodge-flip with the stick. Hold to fly.
BBoost.
XPowerslide. In the air, hold it and steer to roll.
LB / RBAir roll. On the road: side jets.
Right stick · R3Look around · look back.
Y · MenuCamera · recover upright.
